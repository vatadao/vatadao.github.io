Odd O'Clock privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Odd O'Clock is a game made by Thang Dao. If you have a question about this policy, write to daovanthang0604@gmail.com.

What the game keeps

Your progress, your best run, your settings, whether you bought "Remove ads", and when you last saw an ad are kept on your device. They are never sent to us. The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Deleting the game deletes them.

If you ask for reminders, the game sets them on your device, and nothing about them leaves it. You can turn them off under Settings, Reminders, or in your phone's settings.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show them, Google's ad software collects your IP address (which it uses to estimate your general location), identifiers of your device (such as the advertising ID), how you use the game and the ads (such as launches, taps and video views), and information about how the game and the ad software perform. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and preventing fraud, and shares it with the partners who supply the ads. It is sent encrypted.

In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked first whether ads may be chosen for you, and you can change your answer at any time under Settings, Privacy choices. On an iPhone, ads are chosen for you only if you allow tracking; you can change that in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not chosen for you.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your payment details. The game is only told whether you own "Remove ads", and keeps that on your device.

Children

The game is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
