Tour Survival privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Tour Survival is a tennis career game made by Thang Dao. If you have a question about this policy, write to daovanthang88888@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. The game has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own, and it never sends your progress or your player's name to us. The one thing that uses the internet is the optional sponsor video, which comes from Google AdMob (below).

What the game keeps

Your careers, your settings, your best results and which purchases you own are kept on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Purchases

Pro Tour and extra players are one-time purchases made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your payment details. The game is only told which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them after a reinstall. When you buy or restore an item, Google Play or the App Store links the purchase to your store account, which may include your email address. The game itself does not store your email address or send it to us. If you write to us by email, we use your address only to reply.

Sponsor videos (Google AdMob)

After a tournament you can choose to watch a short video to double your prize money. Nothing plays unless you tap to watch it, and if you own Pro Tour you get the bonus without any video. The videos come from Google AdMob. To show a video and to count it, AdMob receives information from your device: its advertising ID, its IP address and the rough location that comes from it, the device model and system version, and how you interact with the video. Google uses this information as its own policy describes: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices about ads

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in the US states whose laws require it, the game shows Google's consent message before the first video, and you can say no to personalised ads. On iPhone and iPad, Apple asks whether the game may track you; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising ID is not shared and the videos are not personalised. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Saying no never takes away the bonus: you still get it when you watch a video, and the videos are then simply not personalised.

Test versions

Test versions of the game keep a gameplay log on your device: the screens you visit and the choices you make in matches and in your career, with a random install number. It never holds your name, your contacts or your location, and it is never sent by itself. It leaves your device only if you share it through your phone's own share sheet, and then it goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

The game is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them or show them personalised ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
