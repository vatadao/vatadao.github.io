Tour Survival privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Tour Survival is a tennis career game made by Thang Dao. If you have a question about this policy, write to daovanthang88888@gmail.com.

We collect nothing

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. It plays offline, and it never sends your progress, your player's name or anything about your device to us or to anyone else.

What the game keeps

Your careers, your settings, your best results and which purchases you own are kept on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Purchases

Pro Tour and extra players are one-time purchases made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your payment details. The game is only told which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them after a reinstall.

Test versions

Test versions of the game keep a gameplay log on your device: the screens you visit and the choices you make in matches and in your career, with a random install number. It never holds your name, your contacts or your location, and it is never sent by itself. It leaves your device only if you share it through your phone's own share sheet, and then it goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

The game is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
